# Outsourcing Companies Training Materials

Training materials for workshops targeting Polish IT outsourcing companies and software houses.
**Audience:** Sales teams, account managers, delivery managers, founders.
**Trainer perspective:** Active IT Director (Fortune 500, Hapag-Lloyd) — buyer of €2–3M/year in outsourcing services.

## Key Theme

> Price is only 10–15% of the buying decision. Most outsourcing companies act like it's 80%.

The training flips the perspective: instead of "how to sell better," it teaches outsourcing companies what actually happens on the buyer's side — scoring models, decision-making dynamics, what triggers a yes vs. a delete.
